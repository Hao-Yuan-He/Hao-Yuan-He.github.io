Short Bio

I earned my B.Sc. degree from the School of Computer Science, Nanjing Tech University. I began my M.Sc. studies at Nanjing University in September 2021 and entered the Ph.D. program in September 2024.

My research includes efficient inference for large language models and the interplay of learning and symbolic reasoning, including neuro-symbolic learning and learning-augmented optimization. I have also been a research intern at Huawei's 2012 Labs and Tencent AI Lab.

I welcome collaborations and discussions. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested. My CV (in Chinese).
