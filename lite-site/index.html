Hao-Yuan He 何浩源

I am a Ph.D. student in the LAMDA Group at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University, advised by Professor Ming Li. My research focuses on neuro-symbolic learning and the efficiency of AI systems, with work published in leading international conferences and journals such as ICML, NeurIPS, IEEE TPAMI, and Machine Learning (MLJ).
